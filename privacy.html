Privacy

No accounts, no tracking

Hokori requires no sign-up or email address. You can optionally publish a wallet profile with a name, picture and links. The site runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

Your wallet is public

When you connect a wallet, the site reads its address to show your balances and fees. Addresses, balances, transactions and published profiles are public. Solana conversion also links the burn to its Solana destination. Hokori indexes public chain data to display coins, activity and profiles.

Stored in your browser

Local and session storage remember your wallet choice and address, pending transaction and conversion records, recent searches and coins, display preferences, and cached market data. These records help restore the page and follow unfinished transactions. You can clear them in your browser; doing so also removes local recovery records.

Connections and service providers

Services contacted by your browser receive connection information such as your IP address. The services used depend on the page and its configuration:

  • Cloudflare, which serves the website and Hokori’s RPC and image proxies;
  • Hokori’s market-data service, for coin lists, prices, charts and holders;
  • Solana RPC providers, normally reached through Hokori’s proxy, and a Solana RPC endpoint during conversion;
  • Coinbase, Kraken and CoinGecko when prices are read directly, including fallback and additional chart currencies;
  • Hokori’s image proxy or Arweave gateways for public images and metadata, and ArDrive’s Turbo service for uploads;
  • your wallet, which has its own privacy policy.

Requests forwarded by a proxy are made by that service on your behalf. Fonts are served by Hokori, without requests to Google Fonts.

Public content and security

Public coin and profile content is processed by automated moderation using Anthropic. Images and metadata published to Arweave remain public even if Hokori hides them.

When you report a coin with its Report button, Hokori’s moderators receive the coin’s address, the reason you choose and any note you write, by email and on Telegram. No wallet or contact details are sent with a report.

Hosting and security services process request information for delivery, rate limiting and abuse prevention. The site also receives browser security-policy reports, which can contain page and blocked-resource addresses.
