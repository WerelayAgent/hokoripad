9. Trading, graduation and transactions

9.1 The bonding curve. A new coin trades on its own bonding curve, a Smart Contract whose formula sets the price: the price rises as tokens are bought and falls as they are sold. The minimum trade is 0.1 SOL.

9.2 Estimates and limits. Before you sign, the Services show an estimate of the result, including the price impact of your trade. You set a slippage limit: your trade executes within that limit or fails. The Smart Contracts, not the Services, determine the actual result, which may differ from the estimate.

9.3 Graduation. When every token offered on a coin’s curve has been bought, the coin graduates: its liquidity moves to a pool on Ekubo, a third-party protocol on Solana, where it is locked permanently by the Smart Contracts, and trading continues there.

9.4 Transactions are final. Blockchain transactions cannot be reversed. 10. Fees

Coins launched through Hokori’s launch factory pay the following fees, which are set in its Smart Contracts and collected by them automatically:

  • Trading fee: 0.9% of every buy and sell. 85% of it goes to the coin’s fee recipients, chosen by its creator, and 15% to Hokori.
  • Graduation fee: 1% of the curve’s reserve when a coin graduates, divided the same way.
  • Launch window: buys in the block in which a coin is created, and in the next block, pay a fee of 99%, to discourage automated sniping.
  • No launch fee.
  • Network Fees are paid to the network, not to Hokori.

The fees for coins launched through a factory are fixed in its Smart Contract and cannot be changed for those coins. Hokori’s share goes to Hokori’s fee splitter contract. The current figures and every contract address are on the Transparency page.

11. HOKORI conversion

Solana HOKORI exists only as a one-for-one counterpart of Solana HOKORI. The entire Solana supply of 1,000,000,000 HOKORI was created once and placed in a locker contract, and nothing can create more. To convert, you burn Solana HOKORI through the conversion program on Solana, naming your Solana address. When two of Hokori’s three independent attester keys have confirmed that the burn is final, the same amount is released from the locker to that address.

  • Irreversible. Burned Solana HOKORI is destroyed. There is no way to convert Solana HOKORI back to Solana HOKORI.
  • Check the destination. Coins credited to a wrong address cannot be recovered.
  • Timing and limits. Conversion depends on the attester services, the networks and their providers, and may be delayed. A burn above the conversion limits in force is held for manual review before it is credited. We may pause conversion, for example during a security incident or where the law requires it. We will make reasonable efforts to credit every valid burn, but we do not guarantee when.
  • No market from Hokori. Hokori does not operate a market for Solana HOKORI. Solana HOKORI trades on third-party venues that we do not control, and we do not guarantee the value or liquidity of HOKORI on any network.
